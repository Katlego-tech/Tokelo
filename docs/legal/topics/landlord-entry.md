The Rental Housing Act gives a tenant the right to privacy during the lease. The landlord may exercise the right to inspect only in a reasonable manner, after reasonable notice to the tenant (section 4(2)). A tenant also has the right not to have their home, their person or their property searched, or their possessions seized except under a law of general application and after a Tribunal ruling or a court order. These rights extend to the tenant's household and visitors (section 4(3) and 4(4)).

Gauteng's Unfair Practices Regulations say a landlord may enter only on reasonable notice, and only to: inspect the dwelling; make repairs; show it to a prospective tenant, buyer or mortgagee, or their agents; inspect it for damage at the start or end of the lease, or once either side has given notice to end it; enter a dwelling that appears abandoned; or carry out a court order. The tenant must allow entry for those purposes, and it must be at reasonable times (regulation 10).
